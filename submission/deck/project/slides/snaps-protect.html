In Protect, a planner enters a proposal and sees its effect immediately: this fifteen-floor design on an open plot beside an eight-metre lane gets a high risk label because ventilation on its block falls by forty-one points. Gemini then searched for alternatives; option B keeps ninety-two percent of the floor area and halves the worst change. The build-out view applies that envelope to the four vacant parcels nearby and rescores the whole area. The app labels risk; it never approves or rejects a building.
