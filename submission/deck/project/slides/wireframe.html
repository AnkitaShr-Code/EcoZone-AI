Instead of a mock-up, this is the real Repair screen after a live search with a ten lakh budget. The left panel shows the plan’s scores, cost, quick wins and ventilation check, with the AI explanation clearly labelled; the map shows priority blocks, new tree rows and cool roofs, with a legend that says everything in words as well as colour. The toggle at the bottom switches between today and the plan at three times of day.
