Both modes share one flow: the planner sets the question, Gemini proposes, and the deterministic engine scores every candidate. Plain code, not the model, judges each candidate against the rules and against a greedy baseline, and stops after two rounds or when nothing improves. Gemini then explains the chosen plan, and an output guard rejects any number that did not come from the engine.
