Additional Details/Future Development (if any)

What we did not measure, and what we build next.

Limits, stated openly

  • Scores are 0–100 screening heuristics; only the heat index was checked (weakly), the others are not validated
  • 612 of 623 buildings use a typology height: OpenStreetMap rarely tags heights
  • Roof albedo is a labelled default; shade is clear-sky geometry on 15 April; one seasonal wind direction
  • Waiting points are hospitals, clinics, schools, colleges and markets: no bus stops are mapped here
  • Not yet measured: Gemini tokens per search, run-to-run variation, live grounding evals
  • Not built: saved plans, Earth Engine layers in the scores, a second city

Next

  • Close the loop: plan in winter, plant with the monsoon, measure next summer with Landsat, recalibrate
  • Field feedback: ward staff log tree survival with geotagged photos
  • Better inputs: measured building heights, roof reflectance, bus stops
  • More cities: one pipeline run each; a second city is the next proof
  • Language: cooling plans in Marathi for ward offices
  • Measure Gemini: repeat runs and token cost per search

15

We would rather show our limits than have a judge find them. The scores are screening heuristics, most building heights are a default, and we have not yet measured Gemini’s cost per search or how much it varies between runs. Next we want to close the loop with real planting seasons and satellite checks, improve the inputs, run a second city, and write plans in Marathi for ward offices.
