07

Everything online runs in one Cloud Run service: the React app, FastAPI, the search loop and the engine. Sliders call the engine over REST and never wait for AI; searches stream progress over server-sent events, and Gemini on Vertex AI only proposes and explains. The area bundle is built offline once per city from OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo wind and computed sun positions, stored in Cloud Storage and loaded at start. Landsat surface temperature is used only to check the heat index in our evals.
