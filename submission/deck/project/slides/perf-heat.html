We checked whether our heat index ranks blocks the way satellite surface temperature does, holding out half the blocks and tuning nothing after seeing the result. On the thirty-three held-out blocks the rank correlation is plus 0.187 with a p-value of 0.30, which is weak and not significant; on all sixty-five blocks it is 0.295. We report it as measured and do not call the index validated; each dot is a block, plotted by rank only.
