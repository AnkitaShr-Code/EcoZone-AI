Solution: EcoZone AI

c. Problem Statement:

Cities have no quick way to decide where trees, shade, depaving and cool roofs would cool the people who wait outdoors, or to stop new buildings breaking the breeze and green that cool a street.

We are team NeuralNodes, led by Ankita Sharma. Our solution, EcoZone AI, answers one question for a city: where would trees, shade, depaving and cool roofs cool the people who wait outdoors, and which new buildings would undo that by blocking the breeze. It runs today on Cloud Run for one real Pune neighbourhood.
