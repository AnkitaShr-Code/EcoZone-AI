The problem

  • At 3 pm people wait at hospital, school and market gates on footpaths in full sun.
  • Cities can’t tell where a small greening budget cools the most people.
  • New buildings that follow every rule can still block the breeze.

Outdoor workers, vendors, patients, students and the elderly spend the hottest hours on footpaths and at the gates of hospitals and schools, often without shade. Repair lets a planner set a budget; Gemini proposes a costed plan and a deterministic engine scores it, including the footpath shade it adds. Protect shows what a large new building does to the breeze and shade, and Gemini looks for designs that harm less.
