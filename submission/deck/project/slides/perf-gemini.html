We benchmarked live Gemini against a plain greedy baseline at four budgets. Gemini won narrowly at ten lakh and lost at the three larger budgets, mainly because its plans left much of the budget unspent and some candidates broke a rule; these are single runs and repeat runs vary. Because greedy always runs alongside, the app shows whichever plan is better, so a weak Gemini run never reaches the planner. In Protect, Gemini’s balanced and climate options cut the worst change on every test parcel, by very little on the mid-road parcel.
